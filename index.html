About PAL PLANNERS

Fresh Vision, Honest Values & Dedicated Event Planning

PAL PLANNERS (पल प्लानर्स) is a young, ambitious event management startup founded on the principles of trust, transparency, and personal attention. We know that every family gathering, sacred puja, wedding, or celebration represents your emotion and hard-earned investment.

Unlike oversized corporate agencies where clients become just a number, we personally oversee every vendor, schedule, and guest touchpoint to deliver seamless and joyful celebrations without budget surprises.

We operate as a value-driven event coordination firm. Our business philosophy focuses on building real trust with families and hosts in our local community before expanding outward.

Target Customers

Families, homeowners, couples, and community organizers looking for reliable and polite event partners without exorbitant agency overheads.

Fair Service Pricing
